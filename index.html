About FODMAP Scores

1–30 (Low): Safe for most people with SIBO/IBS.

31–60 (Moderate): May cause mild symptoms — eat small portions.

61–100 (High): Likely to trigger SIBO/IBS symptoms — avoid or limit.

FODMAP = Fermentable Oligosaccharides, Disaccharides, Monosaccharides And Polyols.
